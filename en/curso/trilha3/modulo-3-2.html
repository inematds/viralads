MODULE 3.2

🎞️ Frame-by-Frame Analysis and Transcription

How to watch an ad in slow motion—on-screen text, shots, visuals—and how to let AI do all that heavy lifting for you in minutes.

1

🎬 What Frame by Frame Means

Analyzing frame by frame means looking at the ad scene by scene, almost stopping the video so you can see every decision that was made. At normal speed, you just "feel" it; in slow motion, you understand it.

🔑 How to do it by hand

Watch it all the way through once. Then watch again, pausing every few seconds to note what changes: the scene, the text, the voice tone.

2

📝 On-screen text

The words written on the video—captions, highlights, price. They matter a lot because many people watch without sound, and the text is what communicates with these people.

💡 Tip

Notice where the caption appears, which word is highlighted, and when the price comes on screen. Write it all down.

3

🎥 The Plans (Scenes)

Each “shot” is one piece of a scene: the person speaking, the product in close-up, hands using it. The the right sequence of shots shows the product in a way that makes people want it.

Face

Builds connection and trust.

Product close-up

Shows the detail that sells.

Result

Proof that it works.

4

🎨 The Visual Profile

The video's overall "look": colors, lighting, setting. In UGC, the vibe of at home, in natural light, and without a studio reinforces “this is real, it’s an ordinary person.”

✓ UGC feel

  • ✓Home setting, natural light.
  • ✓A little imperfection = authenticity.

✗ Commercial feel

  • ✗Perfect studio, cinematic lighting.
  • ✗crystal-clear 4K that no one records at home.
5

🗣️ Transcribe the audio

Transcribing means turning everything the person says into text — the script, word for word. With it in front of you, you can see the speech structure and adapt it to your product.

📊 The shortcut

AI transcribes it automatically in seconds. You don't need to stop and type—it delivers a ready-to-study script.

6

🤖 Let AI deconstruct it

Here’s the secret: you give the video to AI and it breaks everything — scene by scene, on-screen text, transcript, and why it works. What would take an hour by hand, it does in minutes.

✏️ What it looks like in practice

In Track 4, you’ll see the complete workflow: give the downloaded video to AI, get the analysis back, and use that material to create your ad.
