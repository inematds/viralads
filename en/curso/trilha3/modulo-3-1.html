MODULE 3.1

🔬 The 5 dimensions of analysis

Every ad that sells can be broken down into five parts: hook, pacing, audio, narrative, and call to action. After this module, you’ll never watch an ad the same way again.

6
Topics
30

1

🪝 The hook

The hook is the video's first line or image — the first 3 seconds. It’s what makes people stop scrolling through the feed. Without a hook, no one sees the rest, no matter how good the product is.

🔑 Types of hooks that work

  • •Promise/secret: "I’ll show you my trick for..."
  • •Question: "Did you know that...?"
  • •Surprising result right at the beginning.
2

⏩ The pacing

Pacing is the speed at which scenes and dialogue follow one another. Frequent cuts keep viewers engaged; dead time makes people lose interest. The beginning needs to be especially fast-paced.

💡 Tip

Count the seconds between cuts in the top-performing ads. You'll notice they almost never stay on the same image for more than 2-3 seconds.

3

🔊 Audio

The speaker’s voice and the background music. In UGC, the voice conveys the most trust — or ruins it. Remember this golden rule: people forgive bad visuals, but they never forgive bad audio.

✓ A voice that sells

  • ✓Natural and conversational, with human pauses.
  • ✓Current, understated music in the background.

✗ A Voice That Drives People Away

  • ✗Robotic, emotionless, "announcer" style.
  • ✗Muffled or distorted sound.
4

📖 The Narrative

The narrative is the order of the story. Ads that sell almost always follow the same logic, naturally leading people to want to buy.

1
Problem — shows the viewer’s pain point or desire.
2
Solution — presents the product as the solution.
3
Proof — shows that it works (result, demonstration).
4
Offer — makes the invitation with a call to action.
5

📣 The CTA (call to action)

CTA is the “buy now,” “order today,” “click the link.” And it matters where it appears. Without a clear call to action, people like the video but don’t act.

📊 CTA best practices

  • Direct: one clear action.
  • Repeated: appears in the middle and is reinforced at the end.
  • No beating around the bush: "order now using the link."
6

🗒️ Take notes across the 5 dimensions

Create a simple card and note the 5 dimensions for each ad. Taking notes turns "guesswork" into a pattern — after several cards, what repeats becomes your formula.
