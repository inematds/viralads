🔎 Free ad libraries

Meta and TikTok show all the ads currently running for free. Learn to open, search, and filter this treasure trove.

What it is:

At facebook.com/ads/library, you can see all active ads from any brand on Facebook and Instagram.

Why learn:

It’s the world’s largest free source of proven ads. Everything your competitors run is there.

Key concepts:

facebook.com/ads/library · free · public · no login required to browse.

What it is:

On library.tiktok.com, you can find ads running on TikTok, with filters similar to Meta's.

Why learn:

TikTok is home to the "everyday people talking" format. Many viral videos that later become ads start there.

Key concepts:

library.tiktok.com · focused on vertical video · great for UGC.

What it is:

You type in a word related to your product and see ads that talk about it.

Why learn:

It’s the fastest way to find out what your niche is advertising right now.

Key concepts:

Think like the customer. Try synonyms. Write down the good ads.

What it is:

You type in a brand or person's name and see everything they’re advertising in 1 click.

Why learn:

Lets you study exactly the competitors vying for your customer.

Key concepts:

Search by name. See what leaders in your niche are doing. It’s all open.

What it is:

Filters that narrow the search: media type (video/image), platform, active ads only, date range.

Why learn:

Good filtering is what separates “seeing everything in a mess” from “seeing only what matters.”

Key concepts:

Video · active ads only · period · platform. Always start with "active ads".

What it is:

You can filter by country. You can study Brazil or see what's already working abroad.

Why learn:

Many winning ideas appear first in other markets. You can bring them to yours first.

Key concepts:

Filter by country. Get inspired abroad, then adapt for your market.

🚩 The 4 signs of a winner

Four clues that together scream "this ad is selling." Learn to read them and avoid being fooled.

What it is:

How long the ad has been running. 30+ days is good; 60+ is rare and great.

Why learn:

It’s the simplest and most reliable sign that an ad is profitable.

Key concepts:

Check the start date. Time = health.

What it is:

How many times the ad was shown. Very high = a lot of money invested.

Why learn:

No one puts a lot of money into a bad ad. High impressions = a strong bet.

Key concepts:

Sort from highest to lowest. Start at the top.

What it is:

Several nearly identical versions of the same ad running together.

Why learn:

It means the brand has found the formula and is getting the most out of it.

Key concepts:

Count the variations. Many = confirmed winner.

What it is:

Many new ads launched at once across multiple channels.

Why learn:

Often marks the birth of a winning campaign.

Key concepts:

15-20 at once = a bet. Multiple channels = confidence.

What it is:

One signal on its own can be misleading. The strength is in seeing several at once in the same ad.

Why learn:

It keeps you from copying something that only "looks" good. Cross-checking signals gives you more confidence.

Key concepts:

Time + impressions + variations together = a true winner.

What it is:

The "unicorn" is the ad that has survived 60+ days and shows several strong signals.

Why learn:

It’s the best possible model for you to take apart and copy the structure.

Key concepts:

Rare, proven, top study priority.

📥 List of competitors and download ads

Build your list of brands to watch and learn to download any ad with a free browser extension.

What it is:

A list of 20 to 30 competitors vying for the same customer as you.

Why learn:

With a list, you keep an eye on the same brands and quickly notice what’s catching on.

Key concepts:

Write this down in a notebook or spreadsheet. Revisit it every week.

What it is:

Choose brands that speak to the same person you want to reach (age, interests, problem).

Why learn:

An ad that works for another audience may not work for yours. Target the same customer.

Key concepts:

Same customer = useful examples. Define your avatar.

What it is:

Within the library, sort the brand's ads by highest impressions.

Why learn:

The ones at the top are where the money is—the most likely winners.

Key concepts:

Largest → smallest. Focus on the top of the list.

What it is:

Before copying, confirm that the ad has been running for at least 30 days.

Why learn:

Don't copy something that's been around for only 5 days — there's not enough data yet to know if it sells.

Key concepts:

≥30 days before copying. High impressions + time = green light.

What it is:

A free browser extension that adds a “download” button over the ads in the library.

Why learn:

Lets you save the ad video to analyze it carefully and use it as a model.

Key concepts:

Install it in your browser · refresh the page · the download button appears.

What it is:

With the extension enabled, one click saves the ad video to your computer.

Why learn:

You build a collection of the best ads to study and recreate in Tracks 3 and 4.

Key concepts:

Download the winners. Organize them by niche. This collection becomes gold.
