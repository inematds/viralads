MODULE 1.1

🤝 What UGC is and why it sells more

UGC is an ad that looks like it was made by a real person, on their phone, talking to you. In this module, you’ll learn why that “ordinary person” feel lowers people’s guard and converts much better than an expensive commercial.

📹 What UGC means

UGC is the English acronym for user-generated content. In everyday terms: it’s that video where an ordinary person picks up their phone, looks at the camera, and talks about the product like they’re sharing a tip with a friend. No studio, no voice-over artist — it looks like any video in the feed, but it’s a paid ad.

🔑 The core idea

UGC replaces the "look of an ad" with the "look of a recommendation." Instead of the brand talking about itself, someone like the customer shares their experience.

  • •Phone in hand, not a cinema camera.
  • •A person talking, not a brand advertising.
  • •Conversational tone, not commercial-like.

✓ This is UGC

  • ✓"Guys, I found a trick for buying name-brand sneakers for half the price."
  • ✓Person showing the product in their home kitchen.

✗ This is NOT UGC

  • ✗Studio video with a spinning logo and epic music.
  • ✗Announcer with a TV commercial voice.
2

🚧 The trust barrier

When something feels like an ad, the customer’s brain switches into "skeptical mode" right away. It’s automatic: too perfect, too buttoned-up, too corporate—and they’re already thinking, "they’re trying to sell me something." The power of UGC lies in not setting off that alarm.

💡 Practical tip

Before publishing any video, do the "feed test": if it looks like an ad at first glance, trust has already dropped. The more natural, the better.

3

📊 UGC Metrics

This isn't just marketer talk: market data shows a huge difference between ads featuring everyday people and brand ads. Keep these numbers in mind — they explain why this method is worth learning.

📈 Market data (UGC vs. brand ad)

  • ~10x — higher conversion (people who see it and buy).
  • +300% — better click-through rate.
  • −50% — cost per click (you pay less for each person).
  • +73% — engagement (likes, comments, shares).

💡 How to read this

Lower cost per click + higher conversion = you spend less and sell more. It's the best of both worlds, which is why so many people have switched to UGC.

4

👁️ The Viewing Barrier

There's a battle happening before of the sale: getting the person to simply watch. In the feed, your thumb decides in about 1 second whether to keep watching or scroll down. If the video screams “ad,” it’s over.

1s

The first second

People look and decide: "Does this seem interesting or does it seem like an ad?" UGC wins here because it looks like an ordinary video.

3s

The first three seconds

It’s where the “hook” needs to grab attention. Without that, nothing else matters—no matter how good the product is.

5

🪞 Why “authentic” sells

We trust people more than brands. A video featuring an everyday person works like a recommendation from someone you know — that “look what I found” moment that grabs our attention. That’s social proof: word of mouth at scale.

🤝 You sell a recommendation, not a product

When you shift your mindset from "I'm selling a product" to "I'm sharing a recommendation," everything sounds better: the copy feels more human, the video feels more natural, and the customer lets their guard down.

6

🛒 Where UGC fits into your business

UGC works for practically anything that depends on someone clicking and buying after watching a video: an online store, a service, an info product, a restaurant, a salon. If there’s a product and an audience on their phones, there’s room for UGC.

Grab a pen and paper and answer: what is the one product or service of yours do you most want to sell in the next 30 days? That will be your first UGC test in the next tracks.
