MODULE 1.3

⏳ The ad life cycle

An ad is a living thing: it’s born, some become winners, and most die quickly. Knowing how to read the signs of life—time running, impressions, and variations—is what separates the amateur from the hunter. Here, you’ll learn those signs.

6
Topics
30
Minutes
Basic
Level
Signals
Type
1

⏳ 90% die within 60 days

The vast majority of ads stop running within 60 days. The reason is simple: didn’t get results and the company cut it to avoid wasting money. That sounds like bad news, but it’s great for you.

🔑 Why this helps you

The "death" of bad ads does the filtering for you. The ones that survive—the ads that keep running—are exactly the ones worth studying and copying.

2

📅 Time Live = Health

The easiest signal to read: the longer an ad runs, the more likely it is making a profit. The start date appears right in the ad library.

30

30+ days = legitimate

Enough time has passed for the company to cut it if it wasn’t working. You can study it.

60

60+ days = unicorn

It survived the 90% filter. It’s a rare winner — top study priority.

3

📈 Impressions = Money Invested

Impressions are how many times the ad was shown. A high number indicates that the company is spending a lot of money there — and no one invests heavily in an ad that doesn’t deliver results.

📊 How to use it

In the library, you can sort ads from the most impressions to the fewest. Always start at the top — that’s where the money (and the brand’s confidence) is.

4

🧬 Number of variations

When you see many nearly identical copies of the same ad running at the same time, it's because the brand found a formula that works and is squeezing the most out of it. It’s one of the most reliable signs of all.

💡 Tip

Count the variations. 3 versions? Could be a test. 30 nearly identical versions? Winning formula confirmed — copy that framework.

5

🚀 Volume spikes

When a brand releases 15-20 new ads at once across several channels, it's investing heavily in that idea. These spikes often mark the birth of a winning campaign.

Signal A

Many ads launched at once.

Signal B

Showing up in multiple places.

Signal C

All within the same short period.

Reading

Major bet = worth watching.

6

♻️ Patterns emerge with volume

You can't get it right on the first try, and that's okay. After analyzing and running 50 to 100 ads, clear patterns start to emerge on their own—and then you simply repeat and scale what works.

🔁 The real game

Test → observe → repeat the winner. It’s less about genius and more about method and patience. Volume reveals the way forward.
